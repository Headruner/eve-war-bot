## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** to update a running 1.x bot to 2.0 without repeats and without gaps, **so that** the switch from DOTLAN to ESI is invisible in the channel.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a 1.x database (DOTLAN data, no watermark), *when* 2.0 starts, *then* the watermark becomes the highest stored war ID; newer unknown wars are posted, older ones are not.

**AC-2** — *Given* 1.x configuration and credentials files (`dotlan.*`, `report.esi_*`, `dotlan.contact`), *when* 2.0 starts, *then* they are still read.
