## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** to see what the bot would post without posting, **so that** I can test safely.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* `DRY_RUN=true` or `bot.sh test`, *when* a cycle runs, *then* payloads are logged as `[DRY RUN] POST`, nothing is sent.

**AC-2** — *Given* a dry run, *when* it finishes, *then* the real database is unchanged (temporary database in /tmp).
