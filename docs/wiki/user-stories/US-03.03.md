## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** status colours, times in my own time zone and useful links, **so that** I understand the war without opening other sites.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a war, *when* the message is shown, *then* colour is red (standard), orange (open for allies), purple (mutual), grey (ended).

**AC-2** — *Given* a war, *when* the message is shown, *then* dates use Discord timestamps (`<t:…:F>`, `<t:…:R>`) and links go to the zKillboard war page, the DOTLAN war report and both parties' zKillboard pages.
