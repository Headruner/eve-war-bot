## Story

**As a** Discord server admin, **I want** only wars that matter to us, **so that** the channel is relevant.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* `watch_entity_ids` lists our alliance, *when* a war without our alliance appears, *then* it is stored as `filtered` and not posted.

**AC-2** — *Given* `statuses: [mutual]`, *when* a standard war appears, *then* it is not posted.

**AC-3** — *Given* `min_defender_members: 100`, *when* a war against 3 members appears, *then* it is not posted.
