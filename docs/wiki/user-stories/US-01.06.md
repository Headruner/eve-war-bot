## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** ESI problems to be handled without losing wars, **so that** the bot runs unattended, also during EVE downtime.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* reading one war's details fails temporarily, *when* the cycle runs, *then* processing stops at that war and continues with it in the next cycle; earlier wars are posted.

**AC-2** — *Given* ESI lists a war but answers 404 for it, *when* the cycle runs, *then* it is recorded as `skipped` and not requested again.

**AC-3** — *Given* an alliance/corporation detail call fails, *when* the war is built, *then* the war is still posted, with the missing detail left out.
