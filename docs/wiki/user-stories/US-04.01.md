## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** a full war report in the message, **so that** I see how the war is going.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* ESI returns the war, *when* the message is built, *then* it shows declared/starts/finished, ships killed and ISK destroyed per side, efficiency, tickers and defender allies.

**AC-2** — *Given* the war has not started yet, *when* the report is built, *then* no killmails are read and the score shows 0.
