## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** the structures of every corporation of a warring alliance, **so that** I see the whole alliance's infrastructure, not just the executor.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a war where a party is an alliance, *when* the structures are collected, *then* the member corporations come from ESI `GET /alliances/{id}/corporations/` and structures of all of them are counted together.

**AC-2** — *Given* an alliance war, *when* the structures are listed, *then* each listed structure also names its owning corporation (`· Owner Corp`).
