## Story

**As a** alliance leadership, **I want** the number of wars declared on the 1st day of each month, **so that** we can see trends in war activity.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* `bash scripts/bot.sh stats 12`, *when* it runs on the server, *then* a Markdown table lists, per month, wars declared on the 1st (UTC), mutual, open for allies, alliance/corp aggressors, ended, and wars seen/posted by the bot.

**AC-2** — *Given* months before the bot was installed, *when* the table is built, *then* ESI numbers are shown and the bot columns show `—`.

**AC-3** — *Given* ESI fails for one month, *when* the table is built, *then* that row shows the error; other months are still reported.
