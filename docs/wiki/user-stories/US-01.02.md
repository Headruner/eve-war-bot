## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** every new war converted into structured data from ESI, **so that** messages contain correct parties, dates and status.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a new war ID, *when* it is processed, *then* `GET /wars/{id}/` gives declared time (UTC) and status (standard / mutual / open for allies), and both sides' alliance or corporation ID.

**AC-2** — *Given* a corporation side, *when* it is processed, *then* name, ticker and member count come from `/universe/names/` and `/corporations/{id}/`.

**AC-3** — *Given* an alliance side, *when* it is processed, *then* name, ticker and number of corporations come from `/alliances/{id}/` and `/alliances/{id}/corporations/`; members are summed for alliances up to `alliance_member_count_max_corps` corporations, otherwise shown as unknown.
