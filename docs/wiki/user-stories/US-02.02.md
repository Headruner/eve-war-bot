## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** the bot to wait when Discord says 'too many requests', **so that** no message is lost and the webhook is not blocked.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* Discord answers 429 with `retry_after`, *when* a message is sent, *then* the bot waits `retry_after` and sends the same message again.

**AC-2** — *Given* Discord answers 5xx, *when* a message is sent, *then* the bot retries with back-off (2/4/8 s).

**AC-3** — *Given* many new wars at once, *when* they are posted, *then* at least `min_seconds_between_posts` passes between posts.
