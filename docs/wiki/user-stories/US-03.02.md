## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** a picture 'Aggressor VS Defender', **so that** the message stands out.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* `banner_enabled: true` and both logos downloadable, *when* the message is sent, *then* a 640×220 PNG is attached and shown as the embed image.

**AC-2** — *Given* a logo cannot be downloaded, *when* the message is sent, *then* it is sent without the banner.
