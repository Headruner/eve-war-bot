## Story

**As a** Discord server admin, **I want** the bot to check DOTLAN for wars on a fixed schedule, **so that** new wars reach us without anyone watching the website.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* the bot is running with `poll_interval_min: 15`, *when* 15 minutes pass, *then* a new cycle runs and logs `Parsed N wars, M new.`.

**AC-2** — *Given* DOTLAN is unreachable, *when* a cycle runs, *then* the error is logged, the service keeps running and retries next cycle.
