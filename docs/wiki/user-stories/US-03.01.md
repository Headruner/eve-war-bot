## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** to see the logos of both sides, **so that** I recognise the parties instantly.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a war with known entity IDs, *when* the message is built, *then* the aggressor logo (64 px) is the author icon, the defender logo (128 px) the thumbnail, from images.evetech.net.

**AC-2** — *Given* no entity ID, *when* the message is built, *then* the 32 px DOTLAN logo is used.
