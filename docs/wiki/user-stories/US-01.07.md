## Story

**As a** bot operator, **I want** the bot to follow CCP's ESI documentation, **so that** CCP never rate-limits or bans the bot.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* any ESI request, *when* it is sent, *then* it uses version-less routes on `https://esi.evetech.net`, the `X-Compatibility-Date` header and the User-Agent `eve-war-bot/<version> (<contact>; +<repo URL>) python-requests/<v>`.

**AC-2** — *Given* a cached response, *when* it is requested again before `Expires`, *then* no request is sent; after `Expires` the request carries `If-None-Match` and a 304 re-uses the cached data.

**AC-3** — *Given* ESI answers 429 / 420 or the bucket is nearly empty, *when* a request is sent, *then* the bot waits `Retry-After` / the error-limit reset, or slows down below 10 % of the tokens.
