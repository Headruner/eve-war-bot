## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** each war to appear exactly once, **so that** the channel is not spammed with repeats.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a war was posted, *when* the same war is on the page in later cycles, *then* it is not posted again.

**AC-2** — *Given* a war was posted, *when* the container restarts or is re-created, *then* it is not posted again.
