## Story

**As a** Discord member, **I want** to see clearly that a war has ended, **so that** I know it is over.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1** — *Given* a war becomes finished or retracted, *when* the next update runs, *then* a final edit marks it 'ended' in grey and updates stop.

**AC-2** — *Given* `track_days` have passed, *when* an update is due, *then* updates stop.
